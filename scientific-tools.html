SCIENTIFIC TOOLS & INFRASTRUCTURES

Connecting environmental evidence, data and spatial intelligence

Research platforms, geospatial methods and emerging tools that make long-term experimental and environmental knowledge easier to discover, interpret and apply.

My work combines the development of research data infrastructures with spatial analysis and methodological innovation. The outputs below range from operational platforms to reproducible analytical approaches and research concepts under development.

03 / Emerging concepts

From experimental evidence to Living Labs

Research concept / Prototype under development

LTE2LL — Linking Long-Term Experiments and Living Labs

A proposed geospatial evidence-support toolbox to help Living Labs discover relevant experiments, contextualise soil-management evidence, assess transferability limits and identify research gaps together with stakeholders. This is a developing research concept, not a validated operational decision-support service.
